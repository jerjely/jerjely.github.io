Wriggle

Worm tracking from petri-dish videos

Follows a single worm in a dish video along its whole body, also when it coils over itself, and keeps head and tail apart with a small neural network trained on your own labelled frames. It measures length, head and tail angles and speeds, and how straight, compact or coiled the animal is, with built-in validation, single-recording statistics and group comparison.

  • Behaviour video
  • Length, angle, speed, coiling
  • Trained network for head and tail

Opening an installer the first time

The installers are not code-signed yet. On Windows, if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, if the .pkg is blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac shows an Apple M chip or an Intel processor.
