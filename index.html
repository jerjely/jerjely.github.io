Desktop tools I wrote for our retina and superior colliculus work. Each one is free to download for Windows and Mac (Apple silicon and Intel), with nothing else to install.

Imaging

SURF

Spontaneous Undulation in Retina Finder

Developmental retinal waves from calcium-imaging videos. It orients the retina dorsal-up, measures a grid of squares, detects calcium events and follows every wave: how often it comes, how fast it travels, which way it goes and how much of the retina it covers. Single-cell analysis at 40× and group statistics are built in.

  • Calcium imaging
  • Wave speed, direction and area
  • Validated on synthetic waves

Arborist

SC neuron morphology in the laminar frame

Measures where a filled superior colliculus neuron sits: depth from the brain surface and position relative to the Calretinin border of the sensory SC, plus branching, Sholl analysis, the axon initial segment and cell-type classification. Tracing stays in Fiji/SNT.

  • Patch-clamp fills
  • napari based
  • Formerly SC_morph
More about Arborist →

MES Viewer

Femtonics two-photon files

Browses Femtonics .mes files: every z-stack and image with its metadata, ROI measurements, 3D views, channel subtraction against bleed-through, noise reduction with a fast preview, and TIFF export.

  • Two-photon
  • Z-stacks and ROIs
  • 3D view
More about MES Viewer →

Connectomics

ChATlas

Retina connectome explorer

Explores cells in CAVE / Neuroglancer EM datasets (built for stroeh_mouse_retina). Every cell is placed in ChAT-normalised depth, between the OFF (0) and ON (1) starburst bands, using surfaces fitted to the labelled starburst amacrine cells around the region you look at.

  • EM connectomics
  • Stratification vs ChAT bands
  • Needs a CAVE token
More about ChATlas →

Lab management

Mouse Colony

Breeding colony manager

Keeps track of breeding pairs, cages and individual mice: warns when breeders get too old, checks relatedness to avoid inbreeding, suggests pairs and predicts offspring genotypes. Cage cards can be read from photos, and the colony can be shared with colleagues through a Google Sheet.

  • Animal house
  • Pedigree and genotypes
  • Shared Google Sheet

Electrophysiology

Neuropixels Viewer

Open Ephys and SpikeGLX recordings

Shows Neuropixels recordings with traces coloured by shank and ordered by depth. It prepares recordings for Kilosort, lets you curate the sorted units without Phy, drives Open Ephys remotely during recording and sends channels to Toothy.

  • Neuropixels 2.0
  • Curation instead of Phy
  • Open Ephys remote control
More about Neuropixels Viewer →

SC Analysis

Visual responses of superior colliculus units

Analyses sorted Neuropixels recordings from the superior colliculus: receptive-field maps, responses to sweeps, looms and blinks with interactive pickers, direction and speed tuning, and Baden-style functional clustering pooled across recordings.

  • Receptive fields
  • Functional clustering
  • Pooled recordings
More about SC Analysis →

HD-MEA Analysis

BioCam retina recordings

Analyses high-density MEA recordings of the retina (BioCam .brw): stimulus triggers, spike sorting with HerdingSpikes, light responses, oscillations, receptive-field mapping and cross-correlations for gap-junction coupling.

  • HD-MEA
  • Light responses
  • Gap-junction coupling

Rigs

FiberView

Fiber photometry

Records fiber photometry from an NI DAQ or an Arduino with an ADS1115, subtracts the dark offset and computes ΔF/F with several baseline methods, with event averages aligned to visual-stimulus sync pulses.

  • GCaMP photometry
  • NI-DAQmx or Arduino
  • ΔF/F and event averages
More about FiberView →

OptoStim

Laser pulse trains for optogenetics

Switches a laser on and off through an Arduino and a MOSFET module: set the pulse width, frequency, number of pulses and trains, press Start, and every command is logged with a timestamp. A built-in simulator lets you try it without hardware.

  • Optogenetics
  • Arduino + MOSFET module
  • Timestamped log
More about OptoStim →

SyncCam

Behaviour video synced to Neuropixels

Records video from a USB camera while an Arduino samples the TTL sync signal that also goes to the Neuropixels, plus a microphone. Every frame, sample and TTL edge is saved on the same clock. An IR LED driven by the Arduino shows up in the video, so you can check the alignment and measure the camera's delay.

  • Behaviour video
  • Arduino TTL + microphone
  • IR LED sync check
More about SyncCam →

Optokinetic Drum

Four-monitor optomotor arena

Runs the optokinetic / optomotor test in a drum of four monitors: rotating gratings from PsychoPy with settings for direction, speed and spatial frequency, an overhead Raspberry Pi camera recorder with a serial timing link to the stimulus PC, and a lens calibration that turns DeepLabCut head tracking into real distances.

  • Behaviour
  • PsychoPy + Raspberry Pi
  • DeepLabCut-ready

Opening an installer the first time

The installers are not code-signed yet. On Windows, if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, if the .pkg is blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac shows an Apple M chip or an Intel processor.
